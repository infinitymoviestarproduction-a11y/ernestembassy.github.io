Damian Anielozie Okafor
Founder & Chief Executive Officer
👨‍🏫 Professional Educator ✈️ Travel & Visa Consultant 🎬 Film Producer & Screenwriter 💻 Web Developer 📚 Educational Technologist 🎤 Songwriter & Creative Entrepreneur

Damian Anielozie Okafor is a passionate entrepreneur whose career journey spans education, technology, media production, business development, and international travel consulting.

Born into a family that valued hard work and perseverance, Damian developed a strong commitment to education and personal development from an early age. His professional career began in the education sector, where he served as a teacher and mentor, helping students develop academic and life skills.

Driven by a desire to solve real-world challenges, he expanded into technology, website development, digital education systems, and online learning solutions. Recognizing the growing demand for international mobility and educational opportunities, he also established a professional travel consultancy service focused on visa guidance, student admissions, and international relocation support.

In 2013, Damian founded Infinity Movie Star Production, a creative platform dedicated to storytelling, talent development, and film production. The company was officially registered in 2017 and has since grown into an emerging creative brand committed to producing meaningful African stories with global appeal.

Through Ernest Embassy Digital Solutions, Damian continues to bridge education, technology, travel, and creative media, creating opportunities that empower individuals and organizations to achieve their goals.

Founder's Reflection: "Success is not measured only by what we achieve for ourselves, but by the opportunities we create for others. My vision has always been to build platforms that educate, inspire, empower, and transform lives."

Who We Are

Ernest Embassy Digital Solutions is more than a business. We are a platform for growth, innovation, creativity, and opportunity. Our organization brings together expertise across multiple sectors, allowing us to provide integrated services that help individuals and organizations solve challenges efficiently.

Whether it is helping a student gain admission abroad, building a professional website, producing a film, or developing a learning platform, our goal remains the same: To deliver value through excellence, innovation, and integrity.

Today, Ernest Embassy Digital Solutions operates across multiple industries, providing services that range from educational technology and web development to visa consultancy and film production, serving clients both within Nigeria and internationally.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide innovative, accessible, and practical solutions that empower people through education, technology, travel opportunities, and creative media development.

👁️ Our Vision

To become a globally recognized African enterprise connecting people to knowledge, technology, creativity, and international opportunities while fostering sustainable growth and innovation.

Our Core Divisions & Subsidiaries

✈️ Ernest Embassy Travels

Handling professional visa consulting, student admissions, work permits, travel documentation, and international relocation support with personalized assistance throughout the process.

A registered creative division delivering scriptwriting, film production, directing, actor development, music video production, and film training workshops.
