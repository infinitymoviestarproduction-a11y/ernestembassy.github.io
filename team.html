👨‍🎬 Founder & Creative Director
The vision behind Infinity Movie Star Production

Damian Anielozie Okafor

🎬 Founder & Creative Director
⭐ FOUNDER & VISIONARY ⭐

Damian is a Nigerian screenwriter, director, producer, and film trainer with over a decade of experience in the film industry. He founded Infinity Movie Star Production in 2017 with a vision to create compelling stories that inspire, entertain, and address real social issues.

His passion for storytelling has led to the creation of over 20 original screenplays, including political thrillers, crime dramas, and inspirational narratives. Damian's work focuses on authentic African storytelling with international appeal.

Beyond filmmaking, Damian is also an accomplished musician, educator, and web developer, combining creative arts with digital innovation to build a comprehensive creative ecosystem.

🤝 Pioneers & Partners
The people who helped build Infinity Movie Star

Never Matewere

🇲🇼 Partner - Zomba Film Production (Malawi)

Never Matewere is a film producer and creative entrepreneur based in Malawi, dedicated to telling authentic African stories through visual media. As the founder of Zomba Film Production, he leads local productions, talent coordination, and film training initiatives across Malawi.

Never's collaboration with Infinity Movie Star Production has opened doors for cross-continental film projects, talent exchange programs, and shared production resources between Nigeria and Malawi.

His expertise in local production logistics and his deep understanding of Southern African storytelling traditions make him an invaluable partner in expanding Infinity's reach across the continent.

🎬

Emmanuel Fragrance Francisque

🌍 Partner - Ashanti Media Logistics

Emmanuel Fragrance Francisque is an international media logistics expert and creative collaborator with a global network spanning Africa, Europe, and the United States. Through Ashanti Media Logistics, he provides international production support, media logistics, and global creative collaboration.

Emmanuel's expertise in navigating international production requirements, talent sourcing, and cross-border media distribution has been instrumental in positioning Infinity Movie Star Production for global audiences.

His commitment to building bridges between African creatives and international markets continues to create new opportunities for collaborative storytelling.

🌟 Early Pioneers
The foundation team who started the journey

Uchenna Sixtus Uchenna (StSix)

🎭 Pioneer Member / Actor / Drama Performer

A longtime friend and creative partner since 2010, Sixtus has been part of the entertainment journey that eventually led to the establishment of Infinity Movie Star Production.

Together with other talented individuals, he helped form the group known as Golden Steppers, a creative movement focused on drama and stage performance. Although the group later became inactive, the passion for entertainment remained strong.

He later joined the Catholic Actors Entertainment of Nigeria where he participated in several drama productions, stage performances, and creative activities that helped sharpen his acting experience and public performance skills.

As a committed member of Infinity Movie Star Production, he continues to contribute his experience, loyalty, and passion to the growth of the organization. His dedication to teamwork and storytelling has made him one of the respected pioneer members of the production family.
