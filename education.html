👨‍🏫 Why Learn With Damian Okafor

Damian Anielozie Okafor

Educator | Web Developer | Film Producer | Founder

Damian Anielozie Okafor is a professional educator, web developer, film producer, and founder of Ernest Embassy Digital Solutions.

He holds an NCE from Enugu State College of Education Technical and is registered with the Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria (TRCN).

With over a decade of classroom and digital teaching experience, he has taught in primary, secondary, and online learning environments, helping students prepare for WAEC, NECO, JAMB, BECE, and international examinations.
