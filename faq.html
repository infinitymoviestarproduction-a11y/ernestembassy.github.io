✈️ Visa & Travel Services FAQ

❓ How long does the study visa process take? ▼
The processing time depends on the destination country, embassy workload, and how quickly all required documents are submitted. Some applications may take a few weeks, while others can take several months. We guide applicants through every stage to help ensure proper and timely submission.
❓ Do you guarantee visa approval? ▼
No legitimate agency can guarantee visa approval because all decisions are made solely by the embassy or immigration authorities. However, we provide professional guidance, document review, interview coaching, and application support to help strengthen your application.
❓ What countries do you help with? ▼
We assist clients with study, tourist, visit, and work-related applications for several countries across Europe, North America, Asia, and other regions depending on eligibility and program availability.
❓ Do you help with scholarships? ▼
Yes. We assist students with scholarship searches, application guidance, admission processing support, statement reviews, and educational counseling where applicable.
❓ What documents are required for visa applications? ▼
Required documents depend on the visa category and destination country. Common documents may include:
  • International passport
  • Passport photographs
  • Bank statements
  • Admission or invitation letters
  • Employment documents
  • Proof of accommodation
  • Travel itinerary
We provide a personalized checklist based on your application type.
❓ Do you offer embassy interview coaching? ▼
Yes. We provide interview preparation sessions, mock interviews, and guidance on how to answer common embassy questions confidently and professionally.
