
ENGLISH LANGUAGE SS1 – QUESTION BANK (50 QUESTIONS)
📘 SECTION A: OBJECTIVE QUESTIONS
1. Which of the following is a noun?

A. Quickly
B. Beautiful
C. Book
D. Run

Answer: C
Explanation: A noun is a name of a person, place, or thing. “Book” is a thing.

2. A verb is a word that shows:

A. Name
B. Action
C. Place
D. Time

Answer: B
Explanation: A verb shows action or state of being.

3. Which is an adjective?

A. Run
B. Happy
C. Quickly
D. House

Answer: B
Explanation: An adjective describes a noun.

4. Choose the correct pronoun:

A. She
B. Book
C. Table
D. Fast

Answer: A
Explanation: “She” is a pronoun used in place of a noun.

5. Identify the verb:

A. Blue
B. Jump
C. Chair
D. Beautiful

Answer: B
Explanation: “Jump” is an action word.

6. A sentence must always have:

A. Verb only
B. Noun only
C. Subject and predicate
D. Adjective only

Answer: C
Explanation: A complete sentence must have a subject and a verb.

7. Antonym of “big” is:

A. Large
B. Small
C. Huge
D. Wide

Answer: B
Explanation: “Small” is the opposite of “big”.

8. Synonym of “happy” is:

A. Sad
B. Angry
C. Joyful
D. Tired

Answer: C
Explanation: “Joyful” means the same as “happy”.

9. Which is a proper noun?

A. Boy
B. School
C. Lagos
D. City

Answer: C
Explanation: “Lagos” is a specific name of a place.

10. Choose the correct sentence:

A. goes he school
B. He goes to school
C. School he goes
D. To school goes he

Answer: B
Explanation: Option B is grammatically correct.

11. A conjunction is used to:

A. Name things
B. Join words or sentences
C. Describe verbs
D. Show actions

Answer: B
Explanation: Conjunctions join words or sentences.

12. Which is a vowel?

A. B
B. C
C. A
D. D

Answer: C
Explanation: A, E, I, O, U are vowels.

13. Which is a consonant?

A. A
B. E
C. I
D. B

Answer: D
Explanation: B is not a vowel.

14. “He is going to school” is in:

A. Past tense
B. Present continuous
C. Future tense
D. Past perfect

Answer: B
Explanation: “Is going” shows present continuous tense.

15. The plural of child is:

A. Childs
B. Children
C. Childes
D. Childer

Answer: B
Explanation: Irregular plural form is “children”.

16. Which is a sentence?

A. Quickly run
B. Beautiful house
C. She sings well
D. In the school

Answer: C
Explanation: Option C expresses a complete idea.

17. A question mark is used for:

A. Statements
B. Questions
C. Commands
D. Exclamations

Answer: B
Explanation: It ends interrogative sentences.

18. “Dog barks loudly” – “loudly” is:

A. Noun
B. Verb
C. Adverb
D. Adjective

Answer: C
Explanation: Adverbs describe verbs.

19. The opposite of “hot” is:

A. Warm
B. Cold
C. Boiling
D. Heat

Answer: B
Explanation: Cold is the opposite of hot.

20. Which is a collective noun?

A. Book
B. Team
C. Chair
D. Girl

Answer: B
Explanation: A team is a group noun.

Answer: A group of words that expresses a complete thought.

Answer: A word used in place of a noun.

Answer: Tense shows the time of action.

27. Give two vowels.

Answer: A, E

28. Give two consonants.

29. What is a synonym?

Answer: A word with the same meaning as another word.

30. What is an antonym?

Answer: A word with the opposite meaning.
