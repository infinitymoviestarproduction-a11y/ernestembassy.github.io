Damian founded Infinity Movie Star Production in 2013 and officially registered the company in 2017. Through Ernest Embassy Digital Solutions, he combines education, technology, travel consulting, and media production into one integrated ecosystem serving clients both within Nigeria and internationally.

With over a decade of experience across multiple industries, Damian has built a reputation for delivering quality solutions that bridge the gap between traditional services and modern digital innovation.

Who We Are

Ernest Embassy Digital Solutions is a versatile, dynamic multi-niche corporate ecosystem designed to solve complex challenges across diverse global fields. We provide operational structures that balance technical innovation, media production excellence, global immigration pathways, and modern educational services.

By blending our specialized branches together, we give clients, students, and creative partners a centralized platform to launch websites, navigate international travel borders, produce cinematic content, or access cutting-edge cloud-based learning infrastructure seamlessly.
